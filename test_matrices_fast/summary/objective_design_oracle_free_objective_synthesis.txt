Objective-design oracle-free objective synthesis
===============================================

Date: 2026-04-23

Purpose
-------
This pass proposes and tests oracle-free objectives after the repeated
block-only support result.

The repeated-support diagnostic showed:

  - raw block-only gain finds fresh-row support;
  - the strongest fresh-row support is often block-local SVD complement support;
  - the useful signal was fresh support multiplied by oracle consistency;
  - the next step is to replace oracle consistency with an oracle-free proxy.

Four agents independently proposed objective families. Their suggestions
converged on:

  1. full-vs-block combined score balance;
  2. old-row reservoir / history consistency;
  3. explicit current-block SVD-complement rejection;
  4. anti-spike / entropy guards;
  5. held-out future-block consistency as an offline diagnostic proxy.

Candidate set
-------------
Each objective is oracle-free. Oracle labels are used only after ranking, to
measure whether the objective picks the desired candidate on these synthetic
diagnostics.

Candidates per block:

  opt2
  q2_vs_q1oracle
  svd_complement_mgain
  block_svd_complement
  prev_opt2

The important adversary is:

  block_svd_complement

which is the strongest current-block right-singular direction orthogonal to
the selected first slot.


Objective components
--------------------
For each candidate v:

  S_b(v) = block-only combined score
         = combined_score_component_details(A_block, A_block, v, ...)

  S_f(v) = full sketch-inclusive combined score
         = combined_score_component_details(M_gain, A_block, v, ...)

  g_b(v) = ||A_block v||^2

  old(v) = ||old_row_memory v||^2

  future(v) = sum over next H blocks ||A_future v||^2
              with H=2 in this run

  relH(v) = entropy(A_block v)^2 response / log(block_rows)

  top4(v) = fraction of current-block response energy in the largest 4 rows

  align_svd(v) = |v^T block_svd_complement|^2

All share quantities are normalized within the candidate set for that block.


Objectives tested
-----------------
1. Baseline block score

  obj_block_score = S_b_share

This is the corrected block-only score baseline.

2. Entropy / anti-spike block score

  obj_block_antispike = S_b_share * relH

3. Concentration-guarded block score

  obj_block_concentration_guard = S_b_share * relH * (1 - top4)

4. Full-vs-block harmonic mean

  obj_full_block_hmean = HM(S_b_share, S_f_share) * relH

5. Old-reservoir-vs-block harmonic mean

  obj_old_block_hmean = HM(g_b_share, old_share) * relH

6. Explicit complement penalty

  obj_complement_penalty = S_b_share * relH * (1 - align_svd)

7. Old reservoir plus complement penalty

  obj_old_complement_penalty =
      HM(g_b_share, old_share) * relH * (1 - align_svd)

8. Offline future consistency

  obj_future_hmean = HM(g_b_share, future_share) * relH

9. Offline future consistency plus complement penalty

  obj_future_complement_penalty =
      HM(g_b_share, future_share) * relH * (1 - align_svd)

The future objectives are oracle-free but not online deployable as written.
They are useful as offline proxies for whether a candidate generalizes beyond
the block that produced it.


Aggregate n=1024 results
------------------------
There are 4 matrices x 32 blocks = 128 block decisions.

  objective                         q2 wins   block_svd wins   opt2 wins   mean winner oracle_mass
  obj_block_score                   33        74               5           0.320355
  obj_block_antispike               75        30               13          0.740823
  obj_block_concentration_guard     86        22               20          0.780155
  obj_full_block_hmean              80        25               15          0.766742
  obj_old_block_hmean               68        0                21          0.697919
  obj_complement_penalty            106       0                14          0.945174
  obj_old_complement_penalty        69        0                23          0.697028
  obj_future_hmean                  96        12               6           0.902377
  obj_future_complement_penalty     108       0                6           0.978367

Main readout:

  - block score alone fails, selecting block_svd 74/128 times;
  - entropy and concentration guards help, but still fail on
    residual-spiky-shocks;
  - full/block balance helps on three matrices but still selects block_svd
    often on residual-spiky-shocks;
  - old-row reservoir consistency eliminates block_svd, but can prefer
    svd_complement_mgain or opt2 instead of q2;
  - explicit block_svd complement penalty is the best online-style candidate;
  - future consistency plus complement penalty is the best offline diagnostic.


Per-matrix highlights
---------------------
1. static-cex

Baseline block score:

  q2 3/32, block_svd 24/32, opt2 1/32

Best online-style objectives:

  obj_block_concentration_guard: q2 24/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_complement_penalty:        q2 18/32, block_svd 0/32

Future consistency:

  obj_future_complement_penalty: q2 18/32, block_svd 0/32

Interpretation:

  The block_svd direction is highly concentrated / low-entropy here, so
  anti-spike guards work well.

2. diffuse-diffuse

Baseline block score is already fairly good:

  q2 23/32, block_svd 8/32

Most guards recover q2:

  obj_block_antispike:           q2 31/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_full_block_hmean:          q2 31/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_complement_penalty:        q2 31/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_future_complement_penalty: q2 30/32, block_svd 0/32

Reservoir consistency fails:

  obj_old_block_hmean: q2 0/32, block_svd 0/32, opt2 4/32

The remaining winners are mostly svd_complement_mgain, so old-row memory is
not a reliable oracle proxy in this matrix.

3. mixed-tail-sharp

Baseline block score:

  q2 5/32, block_svd 13/32, svd_complement_mgain 12/32

Good objectives:

  obj_full_block_hmean:          q2 27/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_complement_penalty:        q2 27/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_future_complement_penalty: q2 30/32, block_svd 0/32

Interpretation:

  This matrix needs either full/block balance or explicit complement rejection.
  Anti-spike helps but is not the cleanest signal.

4. residual-spiky-shocks

This is the critical stress case.

Baseline block score:

  q2 2/32, block_svd 29/32

Entropy-only and full/block balance fail:

  obj_block_antispike:           q2 2/32, block_svd 28/32
  obj_block_concentration_guard: q2 5/32, block_svd 22/32
  obj_full_block_hmean:          q2 4/32, block_svd 24/32

Successful objectives:

  obj_old_block_hmean:           q2 25/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_complement_penalty:        q2 30/32, block_svd 0/32
  obj_future_complement_penalty: q2 30/32, block_svd 0/32

Interpretation:

  residual-spiky-shocks proves that entropy and full-score balance are not
  enough. The block_svd direction can be diffuse and still non-oracle. Explicit
  complement rejection is required.


Recommended objective candidates
--------------------------------
Best online-style candidate from this pass:

  J_online(v) = S_b_share(v) * relH(v) * (1 - align_svd(v))

where:

  align_svd(v) = |v^T c_t|^2

and c_t is the current block's top orthogonal SVD-complement direction.

Why this is the current best candidate:

  - q2 wins 106/128;
  - block_svd wins 0/128;
  - mean winner oracle_mass is 0.945174;
  - it solves residual-spiky-shocks, where entropy and full/block balance fail.

Best offline oracle-free diagnostic:

  J_future(v) = HM(g_b_share(v), future_share(v)) * relH(v) * (1 - align_svd(v))

Why this is the best diagnostic:

  - q2 wins 108/128;
  - block_svd wins 0/128;
  - opt2 wins only 6/128;
  - mean winner oracle_mass is 0.978367;
  - it directly tests whether support generalizes beyond the current block.

Useful secondary candidate:

  J_full_block(v) = HM(S_b_share(v), S_f_share(v)) * relH(v)

This is attractive because it uses existing combined score components, but it
is not sufficient by itself. It fails on residual-spiky-shocks by selecting
block_svd 24/32 times.

Reservoir objective:

  J_old(v) = HM(g_b_share(v), old_share(v)) * relH(v)

This removes block_svd in the aggregate, but it is unstable:

  - q2 only 68/128;
  - mean winner oracle_mass only 0.697919;
  - diffuse-diffuse selects q2 0/32.

Old-row memory is therefore a useful diagnostic channel, not a standalone
oracle proxy.


Practical design notes
----------------------
1. Normalize within the candidate set.

Raw gain/score magnitudes drift by block and matrix. The tested objectives use
candidate-set shares before mixing components.

2. Use complement rejection explicitly.

Anti-spike and entropy are not enough because a non-oracle block_svd direction
can be diffuse across rows. The objective needs to know whether the candidate is
just the current block's local orthogonal SVD maximizer.

3. Treat future consistency as a diagnostic target.

The future objective is not online, but it gives a strong oracle-free target:
good support should survive into independent future blocks. Any online proxy
should be judged by how closely it matches this behavior.

4. Do not overtrust old row memory.

Reservoir consistency can suppress block_svd, but it can also select
svd_complement_mgain or stale opt2-like support. It should be combined with a
complement penalty and freshness requirements.

5. Full score is useful but contaminated.

Full/block harmonic mean improves over block score alone, but residual-spiky
still breaks it. This matches the prior diagnosis: carried sketch score alone
can preserve stale or misleading support.


Updated objective-design takeaway
---------------------------------
The best oracle-free objective family found in this pass is:

  fresh block score
  x response entropy / anti-spike guard
  x explicit current-block SVD-complement rejection

with future-block consistency used as the offline validation target.

In code terms, the next online experiment should try:

  S_b_share(v) * current_relH(v) * (1 - |v^T c_t|^2)

and possibly a softened multi-complement version:

  S_b_share(v) * current_relH(v)
    * exp(-lambda * sum_j w_j |v^T c_{t,j}|^2)

where c_{t,j} are the top residual right singular vectors of the current block
orthogonal to the already selected slot(s).

The objective should not be:

  - block-only gain alone;
  - block-only combined score alone;
  - entropy alone;
  - full/block balance alone;
  - old-row reservoir consistency alone.

Those all fail at least one matrix in the current suite.
