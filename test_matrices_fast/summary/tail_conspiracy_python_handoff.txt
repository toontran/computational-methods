Tail-Conspiracy Python Diagnostic Handoff
=========================================

Date: 2026-04-22

Purpose
-------
This is a compact handoff for continuing the tail-conspiracy/sketch-bias work.
It keeps only the context needed for next steps.


Core Claim To Test
------------------
The combined algorithm can retain a wrong tail-biased second slot because the
carried sketch B gives that previously selected direction old-energy credit in
later blocks.

The precise failure pattern is:

  opt2 is not aligned with oracle2
  prev opt2 remains tail-persistent
  prev opt2 remains score-competitive
  opt2 wins under actual_B
  opt2 loses or collapses under zero_B
  static-cex does not show rank-2 failure even though sketch influence is high

So the conclusion should be:

  Not all sketch influence is bad. Harmful sketch bias means high sketch
  influence plus persistent low opt2-vs-oracle2.


Terminology
-----------
opt1:
  First optimizer-selected direction in the current block.

opt2:
  Second optimizer-selected direction in the current block.

oracle1 / oracle2:
  Offline diagnostic target directions. They are built from the known target
  top-2 subspace projected into the current sketch-plus-window geometry. The
  algorithm does not know them.

oracle2_vs_opt1:
  oracle2 after orthogonalizing against opt1. This is the fair greedy-frame
  comparison for the second slot because opt2 is chosen after opt1.

actual_B:
  Normal carried sketch context.

zero_B:
  Current-window-only gain context, with old sketch energy removed.

oracle_B:
  Parallel oracle-forced carried sketch context.

Important Output Fields
-----------------------
opt1_vs_oracle1:
  Absolute cosine between opt1 and oracle1. High means slot 1 found the first
  target direction.

opt2_vs_oracle2:
  Absolute cosine between opt2 and oracle2. Low means slot 2 is not the target
  second direction.

subspace_cos1 / subspace_cos2:
  Principal cosines between the selected rank-2 subspace and the oracle rank-2
  subspace. Use these when column ordering is unstable.

prev_opt2_tail_cos:
  Cosine between the tail component of current opt2 and previous opt2. High
  means the tail region persists.

prev_opt2_score_ratio:
  Score of previous opt2 under the current objective divided by current opt2
  score. Near 1 means previous opt2 remains competitive.

opt2_actualB_score:
  Score of opt2 under normal carried sketch B.

opt2_zeroB_score:
  Score of opt2 with old sketch energy removed.

oracle2_vs_opt1_actualB_score:
  Score of the oracle2 candidate orthogonalized against opt1 under actual_B.

oracle2_vs_opt1_zeroB_score:
  Same candidate under zero_B.

opt2_sketch_score_share:
  (opt2_actualB_score - opt2_zeroB_score) / opt2_actualB_score.

opt2_actual_margin_vs_oracle2_vs_opt1:
  opt2_actualB_score - oracle2_vs_opt1_actualB_score.
  Positive means opt2 beats the fair oracle2 second-slot candidate under
  actual_B.

opt2_zero_margin_vs_oracle2_vs_opt1:
  opt2_zeroB_score - oracle2_vs_opt1_zeroB_score.
  Negative means opt2 loses when old sketch energy is removed.
