Forget vs combined/hybrid diagnostic synthesis
==============================================

Date: 2026-04-21

Purpose
-------
Compile the benchmark comparison and the diagnostic-dump evidence behind two
current conclusions:

1. The forget / old-corrected family can overweight the carried sketch or
   current sketch energy in ways that are not aligned with exact SVD recovery.
2. The combined objective fixes one class of forget-score scaling problem, but
   can be misled by diffuse or pooled-uniform tail directions.


Config Compared
---------------
Common benchmark setting:

  n=1024, rank=2, win=32, matrix_preset=fast
  seed=0, shuffle_rows=True, row_shuffle_seed=0
  q0=8, qmax=48, num_restarts=8, maxit=120
  expansion_maxit=8, post_expansion_maxit=80, dtype=float32

Methods:

  forget       score_variant=legacy, basis_selection=greedy,
               rownorm_seed_first_block=True, rownorm_seed_all_blocks=True

  combined     table label greedy-medium:
               score_variant=combined, basis_selection=greedy,
               no combined_rank override, so r1=2, r2=0

  hybrid       table label hybrid-half:
               score_variant=combined, combined_rank_ratio=0.5,
               so rank=2 resolves to r1=1 combined-greedy direction plus
               r2=1 deflated-SVD complement direction

  iSVD / FD    PCA-style streaming baselines from run_joint_warmstart_benchmarks.py

Metric:

  mean_align is the norm of the top-1 exact vector projected into the recovered subspace. Higher is better.

Diagnostic Evidence: Forget / Old-Corrected Overweights Sketch Terms
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Source summaries:

  summary/oldcorrected_score_patch_summary.txt
  summary/oldcorrected_projection_entropy_conversation_summary.txt

Original old-corrected scaling bug:

  The first old-corrected implementation used the old sketch rank as the old
  correction base. On n=128, win=32, rank=2, this made c_old = -4 in the second
  window. The old entropy multiplier became about 16x to 25x on projected
  oracle directions, and the old term dominated the new term by roughly 6x to
  13x.

  That is the cleanest diagnostic evidence for "forget unfairly weighs the
  sketch too high": the score was not balancing old sketch evidence against
  current-window evidence; the carried sketch term was numerically inflated by
  the wrong correction base.

Patch:

  The old rows should be treated as an old-row fraction of the full matrix.
  The row sample estimates normalized entropy, but the sketch rank k_old should
  not be part of the old entropy correction. After the correction, the
  second-window oracle split became comparable:

    oracle v1: old/new = 0.683
    oracle v2: old/new = 0.623
    c_old = -0.4

  So the explosive old-sketch overweighting was fixed.

Remaining forget-family issue after the patch:

  Block 3 in the optimizer-only old-corrected trajectory shows a different but
  related imbalance. The projected oracle directions preserve much more old
  energy and have perfect current-window entropy:

    v1_proj: old E=0.575, old score=0.679, new E=0.376, new score=0.531, rel_new=1.000
    v2_proj: old E=0.578, old score=0.681, new E=0.364, new score=0.515, rel_new=1.000

  The optimizer instead discards most old energy and wins by much larger
  current-window energy:

    opt1: old E=0.148, old score=0.175, new E=0.774, new score=1.054, rel_new=0.890
    opt2: old E=0.072, old score=0.085, new E=0.831, new score=1.143, rel_new=0.921

  Interpretation: after fixing the old-term blow-up, the additive forget score
  can still be energy-dominated by the current sketch/window. It may prefer
  directions that abandon old projected target energy when they gain enough
  current-window ||A_block v||^2.


Diagnostic Evidence: Combined Can Be Fooled by Diffuse / Pooled-Uniform Tails
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Source summaries:

  summary/combined_debug_oracle_span_handoff.txt
  summary/diffuse_diffuse_and_residual_spiky_journey.txt
  summary/kernel_stocks_lengthscale_sweep_findings.txt

Combined static-cex debug:

  In the n=128 static-cex debug run, oracle candidate replacement gave
  near-perfect recovery:

    optimizer_sum=2.0485
    oracle candidate sum=2.0827
    principal_cosines(V_score, Q_oracle) = [1, 1]
    final mean_align ~= 1

  Without oracle candidate replacement, the optimizer-only first block selected
  a lower-score and badly rotated rank-2 frame:

    optimizer_score_sum=2.0485
    oracle_qr_projection sum=2.0827
    principal_cosines(V_score, Q_oracle) = [0.830, 0.269]
    final mean_align ~= 0.431

  This initially showed a search/span-selection failure. Later large-window
  oracle-greedy diagnostics showed that when the objective itself prefers the
  non-oracle frame, oracle replacement is not accepted; in those cases the
  issue is objective-level, not only optimizer-level.

Diffuse-diffuse diagnostic:

  On diffuse-diffuse n=1024, win=32, block 1, oracle and optimizer directions
  had essentially identical entropy multipliers:

    oracle_raw_v1: gain2=0.8306, phi=5.657, rel_H=1.000000
    oracle_raw_v2: gain2=0.8308, phi=5.657, rel_H=1.000000
    optimizer_v1:  gain2=0.8295, phi=5.657, rel_H=0.999997
    optimizer_v2:  gain2=0.8283, phi=5.657, rel_H=0.999988

  Combined was effectively gain^2-only in that first block. With 32 rows and a
  close spectrum, the gain2 gaps are within sample noise, so the choice is a
  lottery among near-tied signal and tail directions.

  From block 2 onward, with 32 current rows plus 32 old-memory rows pooled, the
  failure becomes sharper:

    oracle_raw_v1: gain2=0.832, phi=3.615, rel_H=0.927
    optimizer_v1:  gain2=0.832, phi=4.000, rel_H=0.99998

  The exact signal is globally diffuse, but its projection onto the small
  pooled row set is not perfectly uniform. The optimizer has enough ambient
  freedom to build tail combinations that are uniform over just that pooled
  subset. The combined score rewards pooled-uniformity, not global signal
  identity, so these tail mixes can beat the oracle even when gain2 is tied.

  Diagnosis: pooled-entropy degeneracy. Diffuse tails create a large subspace
  of directions that can look maximally uniform on the current pooled rows.

Residual-spiky-shocks diagnostic:

  Structure clarification:

    residual-spiky-shocks starts from the same residual-panel factor geometry
    as risk-residual-panel: crowding / liquidity residual U columns,
    sector-grouped V columns, structured residual tail, and a close signal
    gap. It then replaces the dense heavy-tailed heteroskedastic noise with a
    sparse row-localized shock on about 2% of rows, column support about n/64,
    spike_amp=0.10, plus a tiny dense Gaussian floor. The final benchmark
    target V_exact is recomputed from this completed A.

    Empirically, at the tuned amplitude the spike is not merely a bad tail
    below the target. It rises to the leading exact singular direction:

      top target:      V_exact[:, 0] = spiky row-localized shock
      second target:   V_exact[:, 1] = residual/event factor signal
      lower directions: structured residual tail plus small diffuse noise floor

    So the useful shorthand is "spiky top, residual/event second, diffuse and
    structured tail below." This is the opposite of static-cex, where the top
    target is diffuse Hadamard signal and the localized spike is a non-target
    competitor.

  On residual-spiky-shocks n=1024, win=32, block 1:

    oracle_raw_v1: gain2=0.664, phi=1.402, rel_H=0.195, total=0.931
    oracle_raw_v2: gain2=0.654, phi=2.928, rel_H=0.620, total=1.914
    optimizer_v1:  gain2=0.649, phi=5.655, rel_H=0.9998, total=3.671
    optimizer_v2:  gain2=0.648, phi=5.655, rel_H=0.9999, total=3.665

  Here gain2 is roughly tied, so phi dominates. The combined objective rejects
  both exact target columns:

    - V_exact[:, 0] is the injected spike. Its row entropy is low, so combined
      penalizes it heavily.
    - V_exact[:, 1] is a factor pulse. Its row entropy is moderate, so it also
      loses to maximally uniform tail mixtures.

  This explains why iSVD beats combined/hybrid on residual-spiky-shocks in the
  benchmark table. iSVD follows the singular values and recovers the spike;
  combined treats that spike as an entropy-bad direction even though it is part
  of the exact top subspace.


Consolidated Insight
--------------------

The two score families fail in opposite ways.

Forget / old-corrected:

  The original bug literally overweighted the old sketch term by using the old
  sketch rank in the correction base, producing 16x-25x old entropy multipliers.
  After that fix, the additive score can still be dominated by whichever sketch
  energy term is largest. In block 3 of the old-corrected static-cex diagnostic,
  the optimizer gave up old target energy and lower entropy because the current
  window energy gain was large enough.

Combined:

  The combined score avoids the additive old/new blow-up by using a pooled
  entropy correction, but that makes it sensitive to the geometry of row
  entropy. It works when the desired signal is row-diffuse and the competing
  tail is row-concentrated, as in static-cex. It fails or weakens when:

    - both signal and tail are diffuse enough that pooled entropy cannot
      discriminate globally; or
    - the exact top singular vectors themselves are row-concentrated, so the
      entropy multiplier penalizes the true target.

Hybrid:

  The r1=1, r2=1 hybrid partially hedges this by taking one combined-score
  direction and one deflated-SVD direction. That is why it improves over full
  combined on static-cex, diffuse-diffuse, and residual-spiky-shocks at the
  n=1024 win=32 setting. But it does not remove the objective mismatch: when
  entropy points away from the target, the combined half still contributes a
  biased direction.


Practical Guidance
------------------

1. Report combined/hybrid wins primarily in regimes where the target signal is
   row-diffuse and the dangerous competitors are row-localized or entropy-poor.

2. Treat combined/hybrid losses on residual/event/spike targets as expected,
   not optimizer accidents. If V_exact itself is row-concentrated, iSVD is the
   more faithful objective.

3. Do not use top-1 mean_align alone for near-tied rank-2 cases. Add the
   rank-r subspace metric before making final claims about static-cex-like
   spectra.

4. If continuing objective work, the natural next probe is an entropy term that
   distinguishes global target persistence from pooled-row uniformity, or a
   gating rule that downweights entropy when the top singular vector is itself
   row-concentrated.
