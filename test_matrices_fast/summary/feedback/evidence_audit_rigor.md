## Cross-Cutting Data-Rigor Concerns

- **Single-seed fragility:** Most diagnostics and sweeps use `seed=0`, `row_shuffle_seed=0`, half_win=32, rank=2. Claims about regimes, dominance, and structural failure should be marked as one-harness evidence unless multi-seed sweeps are cited.
- **Run-version mixing:** The overview combines old S6 synthesis, oracle-aware online, value-only online caveats, GM/D0 frozen baselines, and AB-03 diagnostics. Several exact cos² values are not apples-to-apples.
- **Oracle-aware diagnostics vs operational scores:** Oracle projections are appropriate for audits/ceilings, but operational comparisons must use value-only baselines. The document now knows this but has not fully propagated it.
- **Mechanism breadth:** Several "verified" mechanisms are verified on selected matrices/blocks. That is useful diagnostic evidence, not proof of suite-wide or population-wide causality.
- **Acceptance criteria drift:** Q0/Q1/FAM-01 targets should be rewritten around value-only online and synchronized S6 numbers before any new variant is judged.
