Reading guide:
  - mean_disc_h1_h2 = mean over blocks of 1 - |cos(v_h1, v_h2)|.
                       0 = h1 and h2 solutions agree (high confidence)
                       1 = orthogonal (max uncertainty)
  - mean_cos_both_oracle = mean per-block |cos(v_both, V_exact[:,0])|.
                            Used to check whether high disagreement correlates with
                            the solver actually being wrong on this block.
