Multi-matrix benchmark — combined / hybrid / iSVD / future_hmean_online / future_hmean_evidence
================================================================================================

Date: 2026-04-27


Scope
-----
Same harness, matrix list, parameters, and metrics as
`summary/bench_matrix_sweep/synthesis.txt`. The four existing policies
were NOT re-run — their JSON outputs in `summary/bench_matrix_sweep/`
are reused verbatim. Only the new `future_hmean_evidence` policy was
streamed across the same 17 matrices, with outputs in this directory.

  - same M_gain construction (state = diag(s)·V_prev.T stacked on A_half1)
  - rank=2, float32, --shuffle-rows, seed=0, row_shuffle_seed=0
  - half_win=32 (pair=64 sliding), sliding-mode metrics only
  - 4-way parallel batches with OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=2
  - mixed-tail-sharp run solo, mirroring the existing layout

Driver: `run_bench_sweep_evidence.sh`. Aggregator:
`summary/bench_matrix_sweep_evidence/aggregate.py`. Full per-metric
table at `summary/bench_matrix_sweep_evidence/aggregated_table.txt`.


Policies
--------
  - `isvd`                  pure streaming SVD of M_gain
  - `hybrid`                combined-greedy v1 + deflated-SVD v2
  - `combined`              combined-greedy v1 + opt2 (both from greedy)
  - `future_hmean_online`   greedy v1 + HM-online scored v2 from a
                            5-candidate pool, with rank-2 SVD reframe.
  - `future_hmean_evidence` (new) greedy v1 + slot-2 chosen by
                            sphere-projected gradient ascent on the
                            HM-evidence score
                            (HM_evi(v) · relH1(v) — see
                            `summary/hmean_evidence_score_instructions.txt`).
                            Search basis = rowspan(A_sketch ∪ A_cur ∪
                            A_fut) ⊥ V_default[:,0]; warm starts =
                            V_default[:,1] ∪ online pool ∪ Vbasis_final
                            (≤8) ∪ 24 random unit vectors. Same rank-2
                            SVD reframe as `future_hmean_online`.

The HM-evidence score replaces pool-normalized denominators with
per-block mean-row energies (c_k = N_k / ||A_k||_F^2) and weights the
carried sketch by its rank rather than the cumulative row count
(w_sk = r = 2, w_g1 = w_g2 = N_block = 32). Per the per-block
diagnostic (`summary/hmean_evidence_score_synthesis.txt`) it dominates
the static HM-triplet baselines on the score itself; the question this
sweep answers is whether that translates into streaming subspace
quality.

evi sits between online and the combined/hybrid/isvd cluster on the
tail-dominant matrices: it captures slot-1 nearly as well as online but
extracts only ~half the slot-2 mass (fEx2 0.27–0.28 vs online's
0.83–0.85 on `mixed-tail-sharp/balanced`). On `mixed-tail-soft` evi
recovers some of the slot-2 mass that online misses (0.082 vs 0.019),
but neither method is in the regime of the other tail matrices.

On the strong-signal `etf-basket-basis` evi marginally beats online on
mean_exact_cos2 (0.826 vs 0.814) but loses on final-step metrics
(fEx2 0.889 vs 0.962). The score's sketch-rank-only weighting protects
the slot-1 column at the cost of some slot-2 carry.

On the spiky-residual matrices both online and evi underperform
iSVD/hybrid; evi is slightly worse than online on these — same regime
note as the bench_matrix_sweep synthesis.

The evidence policy is ~15× slower than `future_hmean_online` and
~130× slower than iSVD. Two compounding factors:

  1) per-block float64 sphere-projected ascent with 24 random restarts
     plus the entire online candidate pool plus up to 8 Vbasis_final
     columns as warm starts (≈30+ starts per block × ~1s each on the
     1024-dim search space);
  2) the rowspace_basis(A_sketch ∪ A_cur ∪ A_fut) precomputation runs
     a thin SVD per block.

The optimizer cost is independent of the matrix family (median ≈46s
across all matrices), and the wall-clock difference is essentially
constant across the suite — see `aggregated_table.txt` `elapsed` column.


Summary
-------
- Quality: future_hmean_evidence matches future_hmean_online on the
  mean slot-1 metrics and on a handful of edge cases (etf-basket-basis
  cos2, mixed-tail-soft fEx2) but loses decisively on every final-step
  subspace metric. It wins zero cells on final_exact_cos2 or
  final_tail_mass across 17 matrices.

- Mechanism: the HM-evidence score deliberately rewards balanced
  evidence across (sketch, current, future) windows, and the per-block
  diagnostic (`summary/hmean_evidence_score_synthesis.txt`) confirms it
  dominates HM-triplet variants on the score itself. But "balanced
  evidence on the next-window pair" ≠ "rank-2 carry that survives the
  full sliding stream." The slot-2 vector evi picks at each block
  trades final-step V_exact[:,1] mass for u_sk balance, and the SVD
  reframe inside the next state cannot fully recover that.

- Speed: ~130× slower than iSVD, ~15× slower than online. The cost is
  dominated by the optimizer (24+ starts × per-start sphere ascent in
  R^n with float64). Acceptable for diagnostic / research use; not
  competitive in any production budget.

- Take-away: HM-evidence is the right framing for the static
  per-block question (does this candidate balance evidence across
  three windows?), and confirms its design wins on that question.
  As a streaming policy on the 17-matrix suite, future_hmean_online's
  candidate-pool ranking remains the better choice for the second-slot
  carry, by 7–14 / 17 wins on every final-step metric and by an order
  of magnitude on wall-clock. The two are not interchangeable: evi
  optimises a per-block harmonic-mean target that does not encode
  the across-block carry that the discrete pool plus rank-2 SVD
  reframe in `future_hmean_online` happens to express.

- Open question: the score was designed and tuned to make
  oracle_v2_proj reachable in the per-block diagnostic. The
  streaming carry rule (`make_state` + rank-2 SVD reframe) was
  applied unchanged. Whether a multi-block evidence target (e.g.
  HM over 3+ future blocks, or LOO-style validation) would close
  the slot-2 gap to online without changing the across-block
  carry mechanism is the natural next experiment.


Notes on fairness
-----------------
- The 4 baseline policies were not re-run; their results are taken
  verbatim from `summary/bench_matrix_sweep/*_win64.json` and
  `summary/benchmark_online_vs_baselines_win64.json`. Same matrix
  parameters, same harness binary, same shuffle/seed.
- The evidence policy reuses the existing harness paths for slot-0
  selection, candidate pool construction, rank-2 SVD reframe, and
  state advance. The only new code path is the slot-2 optimizer,
  added to `half_window_sliding_hmean_experiment.py` as
  `future_hmean_evidence` ∈ ONLINE_HMEAN_POLICIES.
- The evidence policy uses no oracle warm starts (those exist only in
  the per-block diagnostic in `hmean_evidence_score.py`).
