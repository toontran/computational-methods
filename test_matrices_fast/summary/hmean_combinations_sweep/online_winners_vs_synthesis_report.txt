Corrected HM variants vs synthesis online-winner matrices
=========================================================

Comparison target
-----------------

The baseline is the `future_hmean_online` result reported in
`summary/bench_matrix_sweep/synthesis.txt`, using the JSON artifacts from
that sweep:

  - `summary/bench_matrix_sweep/<matrix>_win64.json`
  - `summary/benchmark_online_vs_baselines_win64.json` for mixed-tail-sharp

The comparison is restricted to the six matrices the synthesis labels as
counterexample / tail-dominant online wins:

  mixed-tail-sharp, mixed-tail-balanced, mixed-tail-soft,
  etf-basket-basis, diffuse-diffuse, static-cex.

New policies are from `summary/hmean_combinations_sweep/`, with corrected
parenthesized semantics:

  - `future_hmean_triplet_online`: HM(sketch, gain1, gain2)
  - `future_hmean_nested_online`: HM(||[sketch; block1]v||^2, ||block2 v||^2)
  - `future_hmean_pairwise_online`: HM(||[sketch; block1]v||^2,
    ||[sketch; block2]v||^2)
  - `future_hmean_weighted_online`: weighted HM by rows seen.

Interpretation
--------------

The corrected HM variants are not a universal replacement for the
synthesis online rule, but they materially improve several matrices where
online was already the right family:

  - `mixed-tail-balanced`: best corrected variants improve all six tracked
    metrics, with weighted HM best for second-vector/tail endpoint metrics.
  - `mixed-tail-soft`: triplet HM fixes the synthesis endpoint failure
    (`final_exact_cos2` 0.019 -> 0.879, tail mass 0.516 -> 0.132).
  - `diffuse-diffuse`: triplet HM strongly improves the second direction
    and tail mass; the rerun/original online also improves first-direction
    and relerr metrics relative to the synthesis artifact.
  - `static-cex`: nested concatenation is the clear winner on mean/final
    second-vector and tail metrics, reversing the synthesis note where
    combined beat online on the tail endpoint.

Regressions:

  - `mixed-tail-sharp`: none of the corrected variants beats the synthesis
    online result on the important second-vector/tail metrics.  The best
    new result is close but lower on `final_exact_cos2` and higher on
    `final_tail_mass`.
  - `etf-basket-basis`: the old synthesis online result remains effectively
    tied for best; deltas are at numerical/noise scale.
  - `future_hmean_pairwise_online` is weak on this subset despite one good
    static-cex relerr value.  It should not be promoted as a default.

Practical take:

  - Keep `future_hmean_online` as the conservative default for this family.
  - Add `future_hmean_triplet_online` as the most promising challenger for
    endpoint/tail repair on mixed-tail-soft and diffuse-diffuse.
  - Keep `future_hmean_nested_online` for static-cex and balanced-tail cases.
  - Treat `future_hmean_weighted_online` as a targeted tail endpoint variant,
    useful on mixed-tail-balanced but not robust across all online-win cases.
