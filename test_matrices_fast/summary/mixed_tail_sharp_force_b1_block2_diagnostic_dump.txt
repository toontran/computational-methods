Interpretation
--------------
1. b2 is not a clean continuation of the forced b1 oracle frame.

   The selected rank-2 frame has moderate projected-oracle overlap, but the
   second slot itself is already wrong. The forced b1 second direction has
   projection 0.6866 into the selected subspace, yet dot 0.0003 with selected
   opt2.

2. The block-2 columns are swapped/mixed relative to the projected oracle frame.

   opt1 has dot 0.8520 with projected q2, while opt2 has dot 0.5632 with
   projected q1 and almost no q2. This is a frame/ordering/rotation issue, not
   a simple "selected opt2 = oracle2" story.

3. The objective does not strongly reject the oracle frame at b2.

   Under actual_B:

     q1_projected is best;
     q2_projected is only about 1.9 percent below best;
     opt2 is about 0.9 percent below best.

   The badness at b2 is therefore subtle: several directions are very close in
   score, and the optimizer can choose a high-entropy/tail-heavy rotated frame.

4. Orthogonalizing oracle-2 against optimizer opt1 is damaging.

   q2_vs_v1opt has high gain2 but very low phi/relH and only 42 percent of the
   best actual_B score. This happens because optimizer opt1 already absorbed
   much of the projected q2 direction. Once v1 is chosen that way, the residual
   oracle-2 candidate becomes weak.

5. The previous forced second slot depends almost entirely on the sketch.

   In zero_B, prev_forced_opt2 has essentially zero score. Under actual_B it
   rises to 73 percent of best due to the carried forced sketch. So the b1
   intervention leaves memory, but that memory does not hold the second slot in
   the desired column.


Bottom line
-----------
After force-b1, b2 is already frame-mixed. It is not yet the catastrophic late
failure seen around b27, but the clean oracle slot identity is gone immediately:
the optimizer's first vector takes much of projected q2, the optimizer's second
vector points more toward projected q1/tail, and the forced b1 second vector is
not preserved as selected opt2.
