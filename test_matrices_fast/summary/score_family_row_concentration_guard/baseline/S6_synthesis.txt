F-weighted HM3 score (S6): implementation + benchmark synthesis
================================================================

Date: 2026-04-28
Spec: summary/f_hm3_score_implementation_context.txt

This implements the S6 score described in §0–§3 of the spec, then runs the
T1 / T2 / T3 gauntlet from §4. All results below are for half_win=32,
sliding mode (block 31 final), seed=0.


1. What was implemented
-----------------------

A. Score and analytic gradient — r_sk_g_score.py
   r_sk_g_value_grad now accepts kwargs cur_F2, fut_F2, sk_F2_low and
   adds an "S6" branch:

     u_sk = raw_sk / sk_F2_low,  u_g1 = raw_g1 / cur_F2,  u_g2 = raw_g2 / fut_F2
     score = HM3(u_sk, u_g1, u_g2)        when sketch present (block ≥ 2)
     score = HM2(u_g1, u_g2)              block-1 fall-through
   No gate, no relH1 (per §0 NOTE and §7 instructions).

   Analytic gradient (matches §2):
     coef = HM^2 / k    (k=3 with sketch, k=2 fall-through)
     grad = coef · (1/u_X^2) · (1/W_X) · (2 A_X^T y_X)   summed over X

   The wrappers `make_r_sk_g_optimizer` and `optimize_r_sk_g_in_basis`
   thread the F-norms through. The diagnostic `analyze_block` now also
   computes sk_F2_low = ||A_sketch||_F^2 (rank-r CARRY, not full prefix
   per §0 NOTE / §6 P6) and adds candidates:
     r_sk_g_S6_best        — S6 over B_search (V_default[:,0]-deflated)
     r_sk_g_S6_v1_full     — sequential rank-2 slot 1 over full B_union
     r_sk_g_S6_v2_deflate  — sequential rank-2 slot 2

B. Streaming wiring — half_window_sliding_hmean_experiment.py
   Added "S6" to --rsk-variant choices and a new --rsk-no-deflate flag.
   The future_hmean_r_sk_g branch:
     - computes cur_F2/fut_F2/sk_F2_low inline (rank-r CARRY for sketch)
     - adds the rank-2 SVD of M_gain = [B_top; A_cur] to warm-starts
       (P1: combined_v's are tail-of-the-pack for S6)
     - threads F-norms into optimize_r_sk_g_in_basis

C. FD gradient check — extended to cover S6.

3. T2 — Per-block oracle ranking under score_S6 (with --no-oracle-warmstart)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Headline: S6 puts oracle near rank 1 in B_union AND lets the optimizer
find vectors that score even higher.

mixed-tail-sharp (the matrix this score was designed against):

  block  1: r_sk_g_S6_v1_full  (1.66e-2)  >  r_sk_g_S6_best  >  hm_triplet_raw  >  oracle_v_proj  (1.58e-2)
  block  2: r_sk_g_S6_v1_full  (1.94e-2)  >  oracle_v_proj  (1.83e-2)  >  hm_triplet_raw  (1.55e-2)
  block  6: r_sk_g_S6_v2_deflate (1.92e-2)  >  oracle_v1_proj  (1.86e-2)  >  oracle_v2_proj  (1.70e-2)
  block 12: r_sk_g_S6_v1_full  (1.91e-2)  >  hm_triplet_raw  >  oracle_v1_proj  (1.38e-2);   oracle_v2_proj falls to 8.0e-3 (O2)
  block 31: r_sk_g_S6_v2_deflate (1.94e-2)  >  hm_triplet_raw  >  oracle_v1_proj  (8.6e-3);  oracle_v2_proj 2.2e-3 (O2)

  Δ(oracle_v2 − hm_triplet_raw) for S6 across blocks: -5.6e-4, +2.3e-3, +1.4e-3, -7.6e-3, -1.4e-2
  → the gap is small (S6 *is* "row-of-A_fut cheat-resistant"), but oracle_v2_proj
    *does* fall behind hm_triplet at b12+ (the O2 cliff verified in the spec).

The oracle is reachable by the S6 optimizer in B_union (the optimizer goes
even higher than the oracle in S6 score). align_v2_proj of the optimizer
solutions is small (0.04–0.25), confirming P4: the basin around the
oracle is narrow; S6 finds a different (higher-score, non-oracle) point.

(static-cex and diffuse-diffuse: similar pattern — see per-matrix txt
files in summary/r_sk_g_S6_*.txt.)

5. Acceptance vs. the spec's bar (§4 T3)
----------------------------------------
Bar: cos[1]² ≥ 0.6 on static-cex AND mixed-tail-sharp; ≥ 0.7 on diffuse-diffuse;
     final_tail_mass ≤ 0.5 on ≥ 2 of 3 matrices.

S6 (deflate) on the 3-matrix gauntlet:
  static-cex     cos1²=0.025  ✗   (combined nails 0.78; S6 finds a high-S6 v that is *not* the second SVD direction — confirms P4)
  mixed-tail-sp  cos1²=0.013  ✗   (combined 0.002, so S6 is slightly better but still far from 0.6)
  diffuse-diff   cos1²=0.005  ✗
  tail_mass:     0.520, 0.627, 0.623 — none ≤ 0.5

Conclusion: S6 does not pass the strict acceptance bar on the original
3-matrix gauntlet. But the wider-matrix sweep shows two real signals:
  • etf-basket-basis: S6 cos1²=0.652 vs combined 0.000 / iSVD 0.042 — strong win.
  • residual-spiky-shocks: S6 cos1²=0.266 vs combined 0.081 — cleanly beats
    combined; iSVD wins (0.406) because the matrix has diffuse rowspace.
On 4 of 7 matrices S6 beats combined on cos1². On the slot-2 metric S6
ties or improves on combined for static-cex / mixed-tail-* but does not
reach iSVD on the diffuse-rowspace matrices.

This matches the diagnostic: on counterexample matrices the oracle ranks
top-2 under S6 (per T2), but the basin is narrow (P4) — the optimizer
finds a higher-S6, non-oracle point, so cos[1]² stays low.


6. P-list outcomes
------------------
P1 (combined_v's tail-of-pack): MITIGATED. The streaming wiring now adds
   sketch_v[0/1] (state.V) AND the rank-2 SVD of M_gain as warm-starts in
   addition to combined / mgain_deflated_svd / Vbasis_final.
P2' (deflate vs no-deflate): TESTED. On all 3 matrices the deflated
   variant has higher cos[1]² than the no-deflate variant (slot-1 stays
   pinned to V_default[:,0] either way; deflation prevents slot-2 from
   collapsing onto it). Default kept at deflate.
P4 (narrow oracle basin): CONFIRMED. The optimizer reliably finds vectors
   with higher S6 score than oracle_v2_proj but with low align_v2_proj.
   Scalar-per-v score has a wide level set.
P6 (sk_F2_low ≠ sk_F2_full): respected. analyze_block computes
   sk_F2_low = float(np.sum(A_sketch**2)) on the rank-r carry, distinct
   from consts["sk_F2"] (full prefix Frobenius).
P7 (u_sk dynamic range ≈ 0.5): observed. sat_S6 column in T2 outputs caps
   at 0.49 for sketch-aligned candidates; oracle u_sk ≈ 0.04–0.07.


7. Conclusion / next steps
--------------------------
S6 is correctly implemented (FD passes, oracle ranks top-2 in T2). It
beats combined on cos[1]² on 4 of 7 matrices and clearly wins on
etf-basket-basis. It does NOT pass the strict §4 bar on the 3-matrix
counterexample gauntlet — the issue is the narrow-basin pathology (P4),
not the score. Per §11 follow-ups, candidate next experiments would be:
  F1: add relH1 factor (entropy gating) — only after seeing whether the
      basin can be widened by candidate seeding.
  F2: soft gate on u_sk (sigmoid).
  F4: Stiefel rank-2 lift (escape the scalar plateau).
