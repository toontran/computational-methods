# DIAG-02 — Tail-conspiracy forward-replication probe

Generated by `summary/diag02_tail_conspiracy_forward/probe.py` over the §6
mixed-tail set plus `etf-basket-basis`, blocks {1, 6, 12, 31}, half_win=32,
rank=2. Outputs:

- `probe.py` — driver (single pass, ~40 s).
- `probe.json` / `probe.csv` — 64 rows: 4 matrices × 4 blocks × 2 slots ×
  {streaming v_default, projected oracle}.

The probe asks a specific empirical question (per workflow notes for
DIAG-02): are HM3-missed failures caused by **within-window row
concentration that appears stable only because rare rows align in the visible
sample but break on A_fut**?

Concretely, the signature for "within-window row concentration that breaks on
A_fut" would be:

- (S1) `top_share_cur` substantially higher than at oracle, AND
- (S2) `top_share_fut` substantially **lower** than `top_share_cur` (the
  visible concentration does not replicate forward), OR a low
  `topk_jaccard_cur_fut` / `weighted_row_overlap_cur_fut` indicating the
  dominant rows are *different* on A_fut, AND
- (S3) substantial slot-cosine gap (winner v_default is far from the oracle
  on `align_exact_oslot`).

## Test of the within-window-concentration signature

For every score-favoured failure point on the mixed-tail matrices:

- **S1 fails**. The score-favoured `top_share_cur` is **not** elevated above
  the oracle's. mixed-tail-sharp slot-2 b1 v_default top_cur=0.035 vs oracle
  0.041; b6 v_default top_cur=0.033 vs oracle 0.044; b31 v_default top_cur
  =0.144 vs oracle 0.032 (this is the only case where v_default sees a
  noticeably more peaked A_cur, but the slot-2 oracle is essentially the
  diffuse oracle so the comparison floor is rock bottom). Across mixed-tail
  matrices and blocks 1/6/12/31, score-favoured `top_share_cur` is in the
  range 0.032-0.199, while the projected oracle's is 0.031-0.044. A "rare
  rows aligning in the visible sample" failure would require top_share_cur
  in the 0.5-1.0 range; we never observe that.
- **S2 also fails** in the predicted direction. On the mixed-tail set,
  score-favoured `top_share_fut` is **larger** than `top_share_cur` (e.g.
  mixed-tail-sharp b1 slot-0: 0.247 vs 0.035; b6 slot-1: 0.264 vs 0.033),
  which is the **opposite** of "concentration vanishes on A_fut". The
  Jaccard top-4 overlap is essentially 0 across all candidates on
  mixed-tail (a half_win=32 random-ish design gives no shared top rows by
  default), and the weighted row overlap is **lower** at the score-favoured
  direction (0.30-0.62) than at the oracle (0.86-1.00) — but this is the
  consequence, not the cause: the score-favoured v has u_fut ≈ 0, so its
  A_fut row distribution is computed on a near-zero response and is dominated
  by tail noise.
- **S3 holds trivially**. Score-favoured `cos²` to the exact oracle is
  ≤ 0.01 on every mixed-tail slot-2 failure point and 0.000-0.005 on b1/b6
  slot-1 (etf included). But S3 alone is the failure definition, not the
  mechanism explanation.

What the data **does** show, consistently across mixed-tail-sharp /
balanced / soft and at every block:

- **Between-window response asymmetry is the dominant signature**. Every
  failing v_default has u_cur ≈ stream's per-window quota (≈ 1/half_win for
  the rank-1 mixed tail) **and u_fut ≈ 0**, giving `asym` ≈ 1.000 (vs
  oracle's 0.001-0.07). This is the M3 / HM2 imbalance signature already
  documented in §1bis.
- **Carry pinning**. `sa_now` for v_default is 0.79-0.99 (vs oracle 0.00-
  0.51); `sa_next` is essentially 1.000 — the streaming policy folds the
  failed direction into V_state and keeps it. This is the M2 carry-pinning
  signature reproduced in DIAG-05.
- On `etf-basket-basis`, slot-1 b1/6/12/31 also fail (cos² < 0.01) but
  through the same pattern: score advantage 38.25 vs 25.14 at b1 — driven
  by sketch / carry mass that the oracle leaves on the table — not by
  visible-window row concentration. `top_share_cur` (0.08-0.13) is similar
  across winner and oracle.

## Verdict — DIAG-02

**NOT VERIFIED — DEMOTED to unverified hypothesis.**

The probe shows that the HM3-missed failures across the §6 mixed-tail set
and `etf-basket-basis` are not driven by within-window row concentration
that breaks on A_fut. They are driven by:

1. **Between-window response imbalance**: u_cur ≫ u_fut on the score-
   favoured direction, while the oracle keeps u_cur ≈ u_fut. This is the
   M3 imbalance mechanism in §1ter / DIAG-05.
2. **Carry pinning**: state_align_now ≈ 0.8-1.0 at v_default vs 0.0-0.5 at
   the projected oracle, with state_align_next ≈ 1.0. This is the M2 carry
   mechanism already named in §1quart / DIAG-05 and §6 Q11.

The "tail conspiracy" framing in §1bis can stay as descriptive language for
the union M1 ∪ M2 ∪ M3, but the specific within-window-concentration
forward-replication mechanism predicted in DIAG-02 has no evidence on the
§6 set.

## Recommendation on DIAG-02 status

**KILL** the within-window-concentration sub-hypothesis. The signature it
predicted does not appear at any of the (matrix, block, slot) failure points.
Keep the §1bis/§1ter/§1quart split unchanged (DIAG-05 already concluded
M1/M2/M3 are separable; DIAG-02 cannot fold them under a single visible-
window concentration story).

Outstanding work that DIAG-02 was meant to support — namely **FAM-08 robust
row-energy evidence** — does **not** get verifying evidence from this probe:
within-window row concentration is not the failure mechanism, so a robust
row-energy aggregator cannot be motivated on this signal. **FAM-08 is NOT
unblocked by DIAG-02.** The case for FAM-08 would need to come from a
different signal (e.g. a subset of failure points where `top_share_cur` is
genuinely elevated and a robust top-share guard would change the score
ranking, which we do not see here).
