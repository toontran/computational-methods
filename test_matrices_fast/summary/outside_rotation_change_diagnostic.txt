Outside-direction rotation diagnostic
=====================================

Date: 2026-04-22

Question
--------
Check the temporal change of the outside/tail direction itself:

  Do the outside directions rotate a lot more from block to block than the
  oracle directions?

This is different from the previous handoff diagnostic. The handoff diagnostic
asked whether selected and carried subspaces differ. This diagnostic asks
whether the normalized outside residual of the second selected/carried direction
changes rapidly across windows.


Diagnostic
----------
Added:

  outside_rotation_change_diagnostic.py

Raw output:

  summary/outside_rotation_mixed_static_raw.txt

For each block, decompose the second selected/carried direction relative to the
fixed exact top-r subspace:

  v2_in  = P_exact v2
  v2_out = (I - P_exact) v2

Then normalize nonzero components and compute consecutive absolute cosines.

Measures:

  sel2_out_cos:
    consecutive cosine of normalized outside residual of selected v2.

  car2_out_cos:
    consecutive cosine of normalized outside residual of carried v2.

  sel2_in_cos:
    consecutive cosine of normalized exact-subspace component of selected v2.

  q1_cos, q2_cos:
    consecutive cosine of projected QR oracle columns.

  q2_out_cos:
    consecutive cosine of the outside residual of projected QR oracle column 2.

Important caveat:

  `q2_cos` is a columnwise QR-oracle measure, not a subspace measure. In
  near-degenerate/projected settings, the QR oracle columns can rotate
  substantially even when the underlying target span is meaningful. Use it as a
  diagnostic of the frame being compared, not as an invariant property of the
  true exact SVD subspace.

Answer
------
No: the outside/tail direction does not rotate a lot more than the oracle
direction in the failing mixed-tail-sharp case. It is the opposite for the
projected QR oracle-2 column: the selected/carried outside residual is more
temporally stable, while oracle-2 as represented by the projected QR frame
rotates dramatically.

The revised picture is:

  - mixed-tail-sharp selects a stable outside/tail basin for the second slot;
  - the carried sketch reinforces that stable outside direction;
  - the projected oracle-2 frame is comparatively unstable and weakly retained;
  - static-cex also has outside residual movement, but its projected oracle
    directions are stable enough and already present enough for the selected
    frame to converge to the oracle span.

So the tail is not winning because it is rotating faster and exploring more.
It is winning because it is stable enough to be carried, while the second oracle
direction is not stable/competitive enough under the projected QR frame and
combined objective to dislodge it.
