\title{TH-02: Frame-Level Identifiability of HM3 on $\mathrm{St}(d,r)$}
\author{Score-design line, slot-2 / rank-r decision}
\date{2026-04-28}
\maketitle

\section*{1. Problem statement}

Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d}$ be the underlying matrix whose
top-$r$ right singular subspace $V_r := V_{\mathrm{exact}}[:, :r]$ we
are trying to recover online. At a given streaming block we have access
to three row-blocks of $A$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $A_{\mathrm{sketch}} = s \cdot V_{\mathrm{state}}^{\top}
       \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d}$, the rank-$r$ carry of the prefix;
\item $A_{\mathrm{cur}} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$, the half-window
      of new rows;
\item $A_{\mathrm{fut}} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$, the peek
      half-window after that.
\end{itemize}
Define the magnitude-only frame energies
\[
   u_X(V) \;=\; \frac{\lVert A_X V \rVert_F^2}{\lVert A_X \rVert_F^2},
   \qquad X \in \{\mathrm{sketch},\, g_1,\, g_2\},
\]
and the rank-$r$ frame score
\[
   Z(V) \;=\; \mathrm{HM3}\bigl(u_{\mathrm{sk}}(V),\, u_{g_1}(V),\,
     u_{g_2}(V)\bigr)
   \;=\; \frac{3}{u_{\mathrm{sk}}(V)^{-1} + u_{g_1}(V)^{-1}
       + u_{g_2}(V)^{-1}},
   \qquad V \in \mathrm{St}(d,r).
\]
Let $\Pi := \Pi_{\mathrm{row}(B_\cup)}$ denote orthogonal projection
onto the union row span $B_\cup = \mathrm{row}(A_{\mathrm{sketch}})
\cup \mathrm{row}(A_{\mathrm{cur}}) \cup \mathrm{row}(A_{\mathrm{fut}})$.

\paragraph{The question.} Characterise the conditions under which
\begin{equation}
   \arg\max_{V \in \mathrm{St}(d,r)}\, Z(V)
   \;=\; \Pi V_r
   \tag{$\star$}
\end{equation}
holds, or construct an explicit counterexample family showing $(\star)$
fails for any reasonable A1-style assumption on
$(A_{\mathrm{sketch}}, A_{\mathrm{cur}}, A_{\mathrm{fut}})$.

\paragraph{Why this matters.} The empirical kill/continue gate
(\texttt{FAM-01-DIAG}) tests whether $Z_{\mathrm{oracle}} \ge
Z_{\mathrm{winner}}$ on a handful of \S6 matrices and blocks. A
theoretical characterisation says \emph{when} it must hold (or when
it cannot), and this is what decides the score-design path at the
limit. The two gates are complementary, not redundant.

\section*{2. Three target deliverables}

A landed result on $(\star)$ takes one of the following forms; each
changes the next action of the project.

\paragraph{(D1) Positive theorem.} A statement of the form
\begin{quote}
\emph{Under (A1), a spectral-spread condition $C(\Sigma,\,r,\,m)$, and
mild moment regularity on the row distribution, $(\star)$ holds with
$Z_{\mathrm{oracle}} - \sup_{V \perp \Pi V_r} Z(V) \ge \delta(C)$.}
\end{quote}
Then $C$ becomes a value-only certifiable side condition: a window in
which $C$ holds gets the score-family ship; a window in which $C$
fails falls back to iSVD. This is the missing TH-02 (HM3 as a
population lower bound), upgraded to argmax-correctness on
$\mathrm{St}(d,r)$.

\paragraph{(D2) Counterexample family.} A parametric construction of
$(A_{\mathrm{sketch}}, A_{\mathrm{cur}}, A_{\mathrm{fut}})$ on which
\[
   \sup_{V \in \mathrm{St}(d,r)} Z(V)
   \;>\; Z(\Pi V_r) + \eta
\]
for some $\eta > 0$, parametrised by $(d,\,r,\,m,\,\text{tail
shape})$, such that the construction is not pathological (it satisfies
A1 and a row-population assumption that holds on \S6 matrices). This
is Q12 sharpened to rank-$r$ frames, and it kills the entire
magnitude-only path \emph{permanently} (not just for the current
suite). The pivot to Q13 / Q14 evidence features becomes the only
remaining option.

\paragraph{(D3) Sharp boundary.} A separation showing magnitude-only
frame scores cannot identify $V_r$ when the visible-window covariance
$\widehat\Sigma_w$ has sub-leading eigengap below threshold
$\delta(\Sigma,\,m,\,r)$. This matches the empirical
\texttt{mixed-tail-sharp} pathology and motivates cross-window
consistency (Q13) as the load-bearing reliability feature, with a
quantified condition for when it is needed.

\section*{3. Block-1 noise-free reduction (entry point)}

The cleanest sub-question, in which $A_{\mathrm{sketch}} = 0$ so HM3
reduces to the two-term harmonic mean
$\mathrm{HM2}\bigl(u_{g_1},\,u_{g_2}\bigr)$. Consider an i.i.d.\ row
model
\[
   A_{\mathrm{cur}},\ A_{\mathrm{fut}} \overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}
   P_\Sigma,\qquad
   \mathbb{E}_{a \sim P}\bigl[a a^{\top}\bigr] = \Sigma,\qquad
   \mathrm{rank}(\Sigma) \ge r,
\]
and ask:

\begin{quote}
\emph{Does $\arg\max_{V \in \mathrm{St}(d,r)}
  \mathrm{HM2}(u_{g_1}(V),\, u_{g_2}(V)) \to V_r$ in probability as
  $m \to \infty$, and what is the finite-$m$ leak?}
\end{quote}

\paragraph{What is already known empirically.}
On \texttt{diffuse-diffuse} block 1, the optimizer of S6 (which
reduces to HM2 under no-carry) recovers
$V_{\mathrm{exact}}[:,1]$ at $\cos^2 \approx 0.999$ and
$V_{\mathrm{exact}}[:,2]$ at $\cos^2 \approx 0.982$ (overview \S1bis,
M3 verification); on the same block of \texttt{mixed-tail-sharp},
DIAG-05 records a $4\%$ HM-evi gap in favour of a non-oracle
direction. The theoretical question is whether the $4\%$ is
\begin{itemize}
\item a finite-sample artifact that vanishes as $m \to \infty$
      (in which case the population-level $\mathrm{HM2}$ identifies
      $V_r$ and only finite-$m$ stability matters --- direction (D1)),
      or
\item a structural identifiability failure of the magnitude-only
      population score on the heavy-tail family
      (in which case it persists at every $m$, and the construction
      generalises to (D2)).
\end{itemize}

Either answer is informative, and the question is one paragraph wide.

\paragraph{A natural ansatz for (D2).} Take
$P_\Sigma$ to be a mixture
\[
   P_\Sigma \;=\; (1-\varepsilon)\,\mathcal{N}(0,\,\Sigma_{\mathrm{bulk}})
                 + \varepsilon\,\mathcal{N}(0,\,\Sigma_{\mathrm{tail}}),
\]
with $\Sigma_{\mathrm{tail}}$ rank-deficient on
$\mathrm{span}(V_r)^{\perp}$ and supported on a direction
$v_{\mathrm{conspiracy}}$ that has high response in both windows by
correlated tail events but vanishing population correlation with $V_r$.
The empirical \S1ter ``tail-conspiracy'' framing is exactly this
construction; what is missing is a closed-form proof that
$Z(v_{\mathrm{conspiracy}}) > Z(V_r)$ at the population level for
some $\varepsilon, \Sigma_{\mathrm{tail}}$ choice.

\section*{4. Why this is the highest-value theoretical problem now}

\begin{itemize}

\item It is the limit-case version of the empirical kill/continue
gate. \texttt{FAM-01-DIAG} answers ``does HM3 identify the oracle
frame on \emph{these} matrices and blocks?''\ \, $(\star)$ answers
``under what conditions \emph{must} it?''\ The empirical and
theoretical gates jointly decide the score-design path: a positive
theory result + a passing empirical gate justifies shipping with a
value-only certifiable side condition; an impossibility result +
a failing empirical gate kills the family permanently rather than
matrix-by-matrix.

\item It subsumes three currently open / underpowered theoretical
items: Q12 (scalar-score impossibility), TH-02 (HM3 as a population
lower bound), and FB-006 (the underpowered codim-1 plateau argument).
The plateau geometry is a corollary of $(\star)$'s failure mode, not
an independent statement.

\item Q9 (entropy proxy theory) and TH-01 (HM3 max-min stability
surrogate) are already correctly bounded in the docs and do not
change the next action. Tail-conspiracy formalisation (\S1ter) is
informal and depends on $(\star)$ to land.

\item The block-1 reduction is a one-paragraph question whose answer
either way is publishable and decision-relevant.

\end{itemize}

\section*{5. Success criteria}

A landed result must include:

\begin{enumerate}

\item A precise statement of $(\star)$ at the population level
($A_{\mathrm{cur}}, A_{\mathrm{fut}}$ from a stated row distribution),
not just at the empirical-row level. The score family is an
\emph{inductive bias}, not an estimator of $\lVert A v \rVert^2$;
identifiability must be stated for the bias's intended population
target.

\item Either: (D1) a sufficient condition $C$ that holds on at least
3 of \{\texttt{mixed-tail-soft}, \texttt{diffuse-diffuse},
\texttt{etf-basket-basis}\} and fails on \{\texttt{mixed-tail-sharp},
\texttt{static-cex}\}, with a value-only test of $C$;
or (D2) a counterexample family parametrised by interpretable
quantities ($\varepsilon$, tail rank, eigengap), with explicit
$Z(V_{\mathrm{cex}}) - Z(V_r) > \eta(\varepsilon)$ and a numerical
verification on a synthetic instance.

\item A statement of the relationship to TH-01 (max-min surrogate)
and to the rank-$r$ Stiefel lift: $(\star)$ at $r=1$ vs.\ $r \ge 2$
should differ in a way that explains the empirical \texttt{etf-basket-
basis} gap (S6 cos1${}^2 = 0.807$ vs.\ online${}_{\mathrm{VO}}\,
\cos1^2 = 0.969$, a $-0.162$ gap that is the only live operational
target after FB-001).

\end{enumerate}

\section*{6. Out of scope}

\begin{itemize}
\item Optimizer convergence on $\mathrm{St}(d,r)$
(this is INFRA-02 / FAM-01 implementation, not a theoretical
identifiability statement).
\item Per-row leverage-aware weightings (Q4) --- bias-axis-orthogonal
to identifiability and only matters after $(\star)$ is settled.
\item Sample-complexity bounds for fixed $\delta$ --- nice to have
but the qualitative identifiability question is more decision-
relevant first.
\end{itemize}
