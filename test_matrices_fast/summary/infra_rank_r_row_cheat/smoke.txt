Reading
-------

* The per-vector rowcheat columns (rowcheat_v1, rowcheat_v2) reproduce
  the slot-2 row-cheat pathology (raw_g2 ≈ 0.88 dominates HM3) but at
  the rank-r lift their FRAME-level F-weighted HM3 collapses because the
  frame is not aligned with A_sketch (u_sk ≈ 6e-6) or A_cur (u_g1 ≈ 3e-6).
  HM3's "smallest argument dominates" property kills the row-cheat frame.

* The oracle frame outscores the rowcheat frame on both the HM3 (S6) and
  GM3 (S6_GM) lifts, at r=2 AND r=3, on mixed-tail-sharp at b12.

* This is the expected outcome: the rank-r lifts of S6 / S6_GM are
  invariant under right multiplication by O ∈ O(r), so the "row-cheat"
  pathology does NOT trivially survive the lift the way it does at slot-2.
  The smoke run does NOT prove the lift survives on every block / matrix
  — it just confirms the harness produces a meaningful rank-r exploitability
  invariant. Use this on the full §6 matrix sweep when picking up FAM-01.
