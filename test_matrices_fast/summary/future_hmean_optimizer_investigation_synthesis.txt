Future-HM optimizer investigation synthesis
===========================================

Date: 2026-04-25

Starting point
--------------
`summary/future_hmean_retention_diagnostic.txt` showed that projecting the
selected second-slot direction into rowspan([A_w; A_{w+1}]) often improves the
future-HM score, raising the question: why does the optimizer not converge to
those apparently better in-union points?

New diagnostic
--------------
Added `future_hmean_optimizer_diagnostic.py`.

For each block it:
1. Replays the normal streaming combined-score optimizer on M_gain =
   [sketch; A_w].
2. Takes the optimizer's v1.
3. Builds B = rowspan([A_w; A_{w+1}]) orthogonalized against v1.
4. Locally optimizes the single-slot objective
      HM(||A_w v||^2, ||A_{w+1} v||^2) * relH(A_w v)
   inside B.
5. Compares that union optimum against the optimizer's v2 under both:
   - the future-HM diagnostic objective, and
   - the optimizer's actual combined forgetting objective.

Artifacts
---------
- `future_hmean_optimizer_diagnostic.py`
- `summary/future_hmean_optimizer_diagnostic.csv`
- `summary/future_hmean_optimizer_diagnostic.json`
- `summary/future_hmean_optimizer_diagnostic.txt`

Run
---
Eight matrix families, n=1024, half_win=32, rank=2, first 8 overlapping
half-window pairs. Defaults otherwise match the retention diagnostic.

Key result
----------
Across all 56 post-first-block checks:
- 56/56 had a better future-HM point inside rowspan([A_w; A_{w+1}]).
- 56/56 of those better future-HM points had lower combined forgetting score
  than the optimizer's v2.

So the observed gap is primarily objective mismatch, not a numerical failure
of the optimizer to converge.

Summary numbers
---------------
Mean best-union future-HM / optimizer-v2 future-HM:
- mixed-tail-sharp:      13406.8x
- mixed-tail-balanced:    9444.5x
- mixed-tail-soft:       11121.2x
- static-cex:            80455.1x
- diffuse-diffuse:        5835.9x
- residual-spiky-shocks:   546.1x
- risk-residual-panel:      89.3x
- etf-basket-basis:          1.56x

Mean best-union combined-score / optimizer-v2 combined-score:
- most matrices: about 0.43x
- etf-basket-basis: 0.52x

Interpretation
--------------
The union future-HM optimizer finds directions with balanced response on
A_w and A_{w+1}. The streaming optimizer is not optimizing that objective.
It optimizes:

    gain on M_gain = [sketch; A_w] times a pooled row-diffuseness factor

where entropy is computed on A_w plus sampled old rows, not A_{w+1}. That
objective strongly prefers the carried/sketch-supported direction even when a
two-half future-HM direction would be much better under the diagnostic score.

Visibility also matters, but it is secondary:
- For most matrices, the better union point has only about 0.50 mass in
  rowspan(M_gain). That means roughly half of it is lookahead-only structure
  from A_{w+1}, which the streaming optimizer cannot see.
- On etf-basket-basis, the better union point has 0.98 mass in M_gain, so it
  is visible. It still loses under combined score. This isolates the objective
  mismatch even when search-space visibility is not the blocker.

What this says about convergence
--------------------------------
The optimizer is doing what it was asked to do:
- It expands the reduced search basis and usually drives the combined-score
  residual reasonably low.
- It chooses v2 with higher combined score.
- The "more optimal" in-union points are more optimal only for a different
  objective that includes A_{w+1}.

The retained-memory behaviour is therefore structural:
1. M_gain includes the sketch, so old directions remain high-energy.
2. The combined objective rewards M_gain energy.
3. The future-HM candidate scorer can later identify better two-half points,
   but only from a tiny candidate pool; it is not the optimizer's objective.
4. Rank-2 SVD reframing on M_gain then rotates within the chosen span back
   toward M_gain-dominant carried directions.

Next useful intervention
------------------------
Do not try to tune line search or convergence tolerances first. The diagnostic
does not support that theory.

The next meaningful experiment is an objective-level one:
- add a second-slot/refinement objective that explicitly optimizes a
  two-half HM proxy after v1 is fixed, or
- add an online-deployable surrogate for A_{w+1} into the optimizer's v2
  objective, or
- constrain the rank-2 SVD reframe so it cannot undo the future-HM-selected
  direction when the M_gain SVD rotation lowers the two-half score.

Among these, the least invasive patch is to add a post-v1 second-slot polish
inside the online candidate/union proxy span and accept it only if its
future-HM score improves without dropping combined score below a tunable floor.
