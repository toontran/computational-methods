Interpretation
--------------
1. The QR oracle candidate loses, so this hook says "objective/frame issue,"
   not "optimizer failed to find the QR oracle candidate."

   oracle_candidate_check:

     optimizer_sum = 6.634877679379
     QR candidate_sum = 6.604170964137
     verdict = rejected

   The optimizer's chosen frame has higher combined objective than the QR
   projected oracle frame at block 2.

2. The raw oracle columns are actually better than the optimizer, before QR.

   Raw oracle-projection score sum:

     oracle_raw_projection_scores sum = 6.670978020505

   This is larger than the optimizer sum 6.634877679379.

   But after QR orthogonalization, the second oracle basis vector score drops:

     oracle_raw_v2 total = 3.336702471719
     oracle_qr second score = 3.269895415351

   So the key loss is not raw oracle availability. It is the frame/QR
   orthogonalization of the projected oracle span.

3. The optimizer partially captures both raw projected oracle directions, but
   not as the QR oracle frame.

   opt_proj_norms:

     raw v1 projection into optimizer span = 0.615656
     raw v2 projection into optimizer span = 0.876184

   principal_cosines(V_score, Q_oracle):

     0.894781, 0.556387

   This means the optimizer span contains a lot of the raw projected oracle-2
   direction, but the optimizer frame is not close to the QR projected oracle
   frame as a two-dimensional subspace.

4. Entropy is not discriminating here.

   All four score-component directions have pooled_rel_H extremely close to 1:

     oracle_raw_v1 pooled_rel_H = 0.999797665373
     oracle_raw_v2 pooled_rel_H = 0.999691541976
     optimizer_v1  pooled_rel_H = 0.999853116414
     optimizer_v2  pooled_rel_H = 0.999824264721

   The objective is effectively gain-dominated because every candidate is
   diffuse/high-entropy under this block-2 state.

5. Old-row responses are also diffuse and nearly uniform.

   v1/v2 projected oracle old-row responses both have rel_h near 1:

     v1 rel_h = 0.999514822086
     v2 rel_h = 0.999260677406

   So old-row entropy does not reveal a sparse or concentrated pathology at
   b2. The carried block-1 oracle sketch gives diffuse old support to both raw
   projected oracle directions.


Bottom line
-----------
For mixed-tail-sharp force-b1 at block 2, the how-to dump points to a
frame/orthogonalization objective issue:

  - raw projected oracle directions score best in aggregate;
  - QR projected oracle candidate is rejected;
  - optimizer frame scores higher than QR oracle frame;
  - entropy/old-row response terms are nearly saturated and therefore do not
    distinguish the desired oracle frame from the optimizer's rotated frame.

So b2 is not simply "optimizer missed the available oracle direction." It is:
the raw oracle directions are attractive, but the rank-2 orthonormal frame that
the diagnostic oracle-candidate check constructs is not the highest-scoring
frame under the current combined objective.
