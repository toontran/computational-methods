What's new in the per-block T2 dump:

  (i) Per-row "pa1_o2" column: cos² of the single principal angle between
      span(v) and the oracle 2-plane span([oracle_v1_proj, oracle_v2_proj]).
      For r=1 this equals ||V_oracle^T v||² = align_v1_proj + align_v2_proj
      (modulo orthonormality of the oracle frame); useful as a slot-
      assignment-free alignment measure. For oracle_v1_proj and
      oracle_v2_proj it is 1.0 (sanity).

 (ii) Per-block "subspace alignment" table at the bottom: principal-angle
      cos² of each rank-2 candidate frame [v1, v2] vs the oracle frame
      [oracle_v1_proj, oracle_v2_proj]. cos2_a is the most-aligned
      principal direction, cos2_b the least. mean = (cos2_a + cos2_b)/2
      is the Grassmann-style summary.

 Quick sanity reads
==================================================================================

  - oracle_v1_proj and oracle_v2_proj rows have pa1_o2 = 1.0000 (each lies
    fully in the oracle 2-plane). align_v?_proj for oracle_v?_proj is also
    1.0 along its matched slot and 0.0 along the orthogonal one — agrees
    with the principal-angle decomposition in the bottom table.
  - On mixed-tail-sharp b6, no candidate frame has both principal angles
    aligned with the oracle: even the best frame (r_sk_g_S5) tops out at
    cos2_a = 0.65, cos2_b = 0.06. This is the rank-r plateau pathology
    described in §3 / §5 of summary/overview/score_design_overview.txt
    becoming visible for the first time as a SUBSPACE measurement instead
    of two pairwise cos² numbers.
  - The S6 frame (r_sk_g_S6) has cos2_b = 0.00 — its second principal
    direction is orthogonal to the oracle plane, even though its slot-1
    weakly catches a corner (cos2_a = 0.23). Pairwise alignments
    (al_v1pr, al_v2pr columns) fragment this by slot; the subspace view
    gives the slot-assignment-invariant readout the rank-r lift needs.
